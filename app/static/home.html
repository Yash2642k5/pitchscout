What it does

Four questions,
one briefing

Every external fact comes from SerpApi. Nothing else touches the network, and every claim carries the result it came from.

01

Who they are

Positioning, stage and footprint, read off the live web and news results rather than a stale profile.

02

Market direction

Whether the category is still expanding or already saturated, traced through trend and hiring signals.

03

Competitors & price

Who else sells into the same buyer, at what price, and where this company lands on that shelf.

04

Risks & questions

The soft spots in the story, plus what a buyer or analyst should still go and ask.

How it works

Three inputs in,
sourced answers out

The pipeline plans its own searches, normalises what comes back, and refuses to state anything it cannot point at.

1

Give it three things

Company, website, category. The planner turns those into a search set across web, news, jobs, trends and finance.

2

Watch it run

Searches stream in live. Results are normalised, de-duplicated and scored before anything is written.

3

Read the briefing

One page: market, competitors, pricing, signals, risks and open questions — each line linked to its source.
